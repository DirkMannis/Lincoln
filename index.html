How to Play

Lincoln Hold'em Rules

  1. Each player is dealt 4 hole cards.
  2. Betting and blinds work the same as Texas Hold'em / Omaha.
  3. Pre-flop betting round.
  4. Deal the Flop (3 community cards) → betting round.
  5. Before the Turn: every active player must discard 1 card.
  6. Deal the Turn → betting round.
  7. Before the River: every active player must discard 1 card.
  8. Deal the River → final betting round.
  9. At showdown, standard Hold'em rankings apply (each player has 2 hole cards left).

Heads-up only for now. Play until one player has all the chips.
